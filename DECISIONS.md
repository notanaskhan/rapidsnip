# Decisions

Choices made during the build that PLAN.md doesn't cover.

- **2026-11-18 · Project lives in `~/Documents/Clipper`**, not `~/gta-clipper`. The plan file was renamed to `PLAN.md`.
- **2026-11-18 · Example channel ships with an empty `permission`** so nothing is recorded until a real, permitted channel is added. The test tools (`clipper.chat`, `tools/test_live.py`) accept any channel name because they only smoke-test locally and never produce postable clips.
- **2026-11-18 · Per-channel overrides are flat keys** on the channel entry (any detector or clip setting, e.g. `ratio_threshold`, `chat_delay_seconds`), merged over the global sections.
- **2026-11-18 · Added `min_free_gb` (default 20)** to config for the startup disk check.
- **2026-11-18 · No `--twitch-disable-ads`.** Streamlink 8 removed the flag; it filters ad segments by default. Added `--twitch-supported-codecs h264` so Twitch never serves HEVC/AV1.
- **2026-11-18 · Segment wall times come from a per-session anchor**, not raw arrival time: `wall = stream_time + offset`, where `offset` is the smallest `arrival - stream_end` seen in the session. Raw arrival times bunch up when Streamlink delivers a burst (startup, after a stall), which would make segments overlap. If the stream clock slips >5 s for 10 segments in a row, a new anchor starts.
- **2026-11-18 · Clip locks are time ranges**, not segment lists, so a lock also protects segments that haven't been recorded yet.
- **2026-11-18 · Recorder deletes session folders from earlier runs at startup.** The buffer is only useful while the app is running.
- **2026-11-18 · Restarts lose ~7 s of video** (2 s delay + Streamlink startup). Measured on xqc. After 3 quick failures in a row the delay rises to 15 s.
- **2026-11-18 · Pause rules narrowed.** The plan pauses after any gift-sub event, but xqc gets a small gift bomb every 30-60 s, which kept the detector paused almost all the time. Now: pause after a raid, a mystery gift of 10+ subs, or 5+ sub/resub/standalone-gift events within 30 s. Gifts that belong to a mystery gift (`community-gift-id`) only count through the mystery gift's size.
- **2026-11-18 · Baseline excludes only windows that are part of a trigger or a pause, and never during warm-up.** Excluding every above-threshold window let an empty starting baseline (floor 1) lock out all windows forever.
- **2026-11-18 · Onset uses a per-bin threshold** of `1.5 * baseline / bins_per_window` (the baseline is per 10 s window, bins are 2 s), walking back at most 30 s through an unbroken run of hot bins.
- **2026-11-18 · Triggers are emitted early and updated.** The detector emits a trigger as soon as it fires (`final=False`), re-emits it as later hot windows extend it, and emits `final=True` once chat has been quiet for `merge_gap_seconds` or the clip hits `max_length_seconds`. Cooldown counts from the last hot window of the previous trigger.
- **2026-11-18 · Replay splits logs at silences over 60 s** and starts a fresh detector (with warm-up) after each, matching how the live app starts a detector when a channel goes live.
- **2026-11-18 · A clip is cut from one contiguous run of segments.** Concatenating across a restart gap would shift everything after the gap, so the job picks the run covering the most of the window and trims to it; the 20% missing rule applies to that run.
- **2026-11-18 · Cutter seeks after the input (`-ss` after `-i`) on the concat list** for frame-exact starts. Measured on xqc: 30 s 1080p60 clip in ~9 s, first frame matches the source at +0.000 s.
- **2026-11-18 · Whisper hallucination filter.** Whisper writes "Thank you." over silence. Words below probability 0.35, segments with no_speech_prob > 0.6 and avg_logprob < -1, and stock phrases ("thank you", "thanks for watching", ...) are dropped; fewer than 2 surviving words = no captions. Also `condition_on_previous_text=False` and `hallucination_silence_threshold=1.0`. Verified: pink noise + vibrato tone and pure silence both give 0 words.
- **2026-11-18 · Captions font is Arial Black** from `/System/Library/Fonts/Supplemental`, pinned with the subtitles filter's `fontsdir` so libass never falls back to another font.
- **2026-11-18 · A caption stays up until the next one starts** unless there is a pause over 0.6 s (then it holds 0.25 s past its last word). A pause over 0.6 s also starts a new caption even if it has fewer than `words_per_caption` words.
- **2026-11-18 · Executables are resolved from the venv first** (`streamlink` next to `sys.executable`), so the app works whether started with `uv run` or `.venv/bin/python`.
- **2026-11-18 · The worker leaves the terminal's process group and ignores SIGINT**, so Ctrl+C lets the current clip finish; it exits on its own if the supervisor dies.
- **2026-11-18 · Vertical and horizontal render in one FFmpeg pass** (one decode, `split`, two VideoToolbox encodes: vertical 10 Mb/s, horizontal 8 Mb/s).
- **2026-11-18 · Crop boxes are in 1920x1080 pixels** and scaled by `source_width / 1920` when the stream comes in at another resolution (e.g. the 720p fallback). `tools/calibrate.py` always grabs frames scaled to 1920x1080.
- **2026-11-18 · Caption placement.** With a facecam: centred ~90 px below the band boundary (top of the gameplay band). Without: centred ~110 px below the video, in the blurred band, so captions never cover the game.
- **2026-11-18 · IShowSpeed calibrated** from a live frame: facecam [0, 460, 388, 242], gameplay [400, 0, 1300, 1080] (drops the facecam column and his on-screen chat).
- **2026-11-18 · Per-clip processing is ~32-35 s** (cut 9 + captions 4-7 + render 18) while recording runs alongside. Slightly over the 30 s target; the obvious saving is to skip the intermediate `horizontal_raw.mp4` encode and render both outputs straight from the segments. Left for M9.
- **2026-11-18 · Review page is a static `clips/index.html`**, rebuilt by the worker after every clip (written to a temp file then renamed, so a browser never sees half a page) and once at startup. Vertical players use `vertical.mp4#t=0.5` instead of a poster, since `thumb.jpg` is 16:9. Copy buttons try `navigator.clipboard` and fall back to `execCommand("copy")` for `file://` pages.
- **2026-11-18 · Notification text:** title "New clip ready", message "<display name> <ratio>x spike", subtitle = suggested title.
- **2026-11-18 · Reaction list extended** with LOL, LOLW, ICANT, MegaLUL, KEKL, 😭, OMG, D:, monkaW. Replay of 41 min of xqc chat went from 0 triggers (peaks 2.7-3.0x, all "LOL"/"D:"/"MegaLUL" reactions the list didn't know) to 5 (7.4/hour); IShowSpeed unchanged (2 triggers, 6.2/hour). Replay of IShowSpeed reproduced the live app's two triggers exactly.
- **2026-11-18 · `tools/replay.py --peaks N`** lists the biggest windows with what they needed to fire, for tuning thresholds.
- **2026-11-18 · Onset uses a per-bin baseline** (median and MAD of non-excluded 2 s bin scores over the last `baseline_minutes`): a bin counts as part of the spike only if it beats both 1.5x the bin median and median + 3 MAD. The earlier `1.5 * window_baseline / 5` rule let ordinary noisy bins through, pulling onset up to 12 s too early (caught by the merge test once "lol" became a reaction). Replaces the onset note above.
- **2026-11-18 · The supervisor installs its own SIGINT/SIGTERM handlers** (`loop.add_signal_handler`). A process started in the background from a script inherits "ignore SIGINT", so the first M5 Ctrl+C test did nothing and had to be killed, leaving Streamlink/FFmpeg running. A second signal exits immediately. Streamlink and FFmpeg run in their own session so a terminal Ctrl+C reaches only the app, which stops them in order. Verified: SIGINT -> clean exit in 4 s, no leftover processes.
- **2026-11-18 · Kai Cenat calibrated:** facecam [0, 432, 392, 220]; gameplay [400, 90, 1300, 990] (skips facecam, his on-screen chat, the viewer counter and the game's compass bar).
- **2026-11-18 · Clips render straight from the segments** (M9): concat list -> `setpts`/`trim` in the filter graph -> vertical + horizontal in one encode; Whisper's audio comes from the same window with `atrim`. No `horizontal_raw.mp4` any more; `thumb.jpg` comes from `horizontal.mp4`. Measured on IShowSpeed with nothing else encoding: 23.8 s per 30 s clip (was ~33 s). First frame within 1 frame (1/60 s) of the requested start on IShowSpeed and Kai Cenat. `tools/test_cut.py` still checks the original cutter.
- **2026-11-18 · Status line every 5 minutes** in `data/clipper.log` (buffer, segments, restarts, score/baseline, clips, queue, chat state), plus `tools/monitor.sh` for process memory and disk during soak tests.
